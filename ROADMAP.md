# Roadmap

## Included baseline

Author-controlled governance, evidence and canon separation, interview and rewrite protocols, book scaffolding, character and story templates, scene and series tracking, contributor guidance, and dependency-free local checks.

## Possible future work - not implemented

Validated conversation importers with format-specific completeness reporting; richer timeline and relationship visualization; additional manuscript-format exporters; schema-aware record editing; accessibility and translation improvements; optional integrations with writing applications.

These are discussion areas, not promised delivery dates. Proposals must preserve source fidelity, privacy, author approvals, and operation-specific permissions. No unattended canon promotion, automatic manuscript ingestion, or automatic publication is planned.
