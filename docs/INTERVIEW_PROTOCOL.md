# Creator interview protocol

Before preparing questions, inspect authorized canon, decision records, prior interviews, review queues, and open questions. Do not inspect a manuscript inbox merely because an interview is requested. Match the questions to the author's actual narrative scale.

Start with a clear header: project, creator, interview ID, domain, purpose, source type, repository branch and exact commit, authorized sources, contradictions, and handling instructions.

Default to approximately twenty non-repeating questions presented together. Use [the interview template](../09_Tools/Templates/Creator_Interview.md). The author can choose another number or cadence. Ask about specific missing information and causal conflicts rather than filling a questionnaire with irrelevant biography.

Each numbered answer may begin with one primary label: Established, Working idea, Unknown, Intentional mystery, or Not relevant. Preserve mixed settled and tentative content under separate sublabels. Missing labels remain unclassified until clarified; do not silently assign a status for the author.

Wait for the author to finish. When the author requests no interim analysis, only acknowledge receipt when asked. Do not repeatedly re-ask answered questions or expand the agreed interview unilaterally.

At completion, preserve available visible messages in order, including questions, answer labels, corrections, and original wording. Distinguish exact transcript from any editorial summary. State incomplete capture or omitted attachments honestly. Produce DOCX or another requested export only with a supported document tool; no transcript exporter is bundled here.

Interview completion does not settle every question or approve canon. Preservation, extraction, review, and promotion remain separately authorized operations.
