# Licensing and ownership

The [MIT License](../LICENSE) applies to the framework code, documentation, and templates distributed in this repository. Keep its copyright and permission notice with copies or substantial portions of that material.

Using these tools does not require you to publish independently authored stories under MIT. Keep original manuscripts and other creative assets in a separate repository or clearly identified collection with their own rights statement. Do not assume that uploading a third-party image, research source, or manuscript gives you permission to redistribute it.

Framework contributions should contain material you have the right to submit under the repository license. Do not contribute copyrighted fictional universes, private conversations, or substantial copied book passages as sample data.

This guide clarifies the intended separation of framework and creative work; it does not add restrictions to the MIT License or settle ownership of third-party material.
