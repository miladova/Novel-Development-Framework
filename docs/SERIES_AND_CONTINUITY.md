# Series and continuity

Use one repository for one connected fictional universe. Separate unrelated universes. Put each book in `05_Books/BOOK-###/`; share approved facts through `02_Canon/` rather than copying conflicting versions into book folders.

For every record, specify whether it applies to the whole universe, a particular era, or one book. Distinguish objective facts from what a character believes, what a narrator reports, and what the reader currently knows.

Maintain [Series Map](../04_Story/Series_Map.md), [Timeline](../04_Story/Timeline.md), [Plot Threads](../04_Story/Plot_Threads.md), and [Continuity Tracker](../04_Story/Continuity_Tracker.md). Use stable book, scene, event, character, and thread IDs. A scene can reference canon without turning its entire prose into canon.

Before changing an established fact, list the affected characters, dates, places, scenes, later books, and published editions. Decide whether this is a correction, a deliberate retcon, an unreliable account, or an intentional mystery. Ask the author; do not choose silently. Preserve the earlier record and its decision history.

Review event order separately from narrative order. Check ages, travel time, knowledge available to each viewpoint, relationship changes, setup/payoff timing, and promises inherited by later books. Numerical dates are optional; a documented relative order is preferable to invented precision.
