# Historical and active manuscript workflow

- `_inbox/manuscripts/` holds unprocessed, unchanged source drafts; no automatic reading or import.
- Dry-inspect only a *named* manuscript and only with author authorization; record identity, format, filesize, SHA-256 and proposed handling.
- After separate author approval, preserve source into `archive/relics/manuscripts/<RELIC-ID>/original/` with checksum, metadata and manifest.
- Never overwrite or merge historical originals. Unknown writing dates and family relationships stay unknown. A filename can suggest a version but does not establish authorial provenance.
- `05_Books/<Book>/Chapters/` contains active drafts. Historic evidence is never edited to match newer continuity.
- Extraction, comparing, drafting, canon approval and publication are distinct operations.
