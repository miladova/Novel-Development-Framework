# Community expectations

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique proposed work and ideas rather than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sharing another person's private creative work are not acceptable.

Give clear, constructive feedback and respect different genres, creative traditions, accessibility needs, and levels of technical experience. Do not pressure an author to publish private work or adopt your creative preferences.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate public contributions and limit participation. For a conduct concern, contact a maintainer through a private channel they have made available; do not post private evidence or personal details in a public issue. If no private channel is available, ask for one without disclosing sensitive information.
