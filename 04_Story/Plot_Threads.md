# Plot threads, promises, and payoffs

Mark each thread as Proposed, Seeded, Developing, Resolved, Deliberately unresolved, or Rejected. A deliberate mystery is not an accidental dropped thread. Record who knows the answer and when the reader can learn it. Link the [plot-thread template](../09_Tools/Templates/Plot_Thread.md) for complex threads.
