# Canon policy

Only `02_Canon/` defines author-approved fictional facts. Development notes, research, manuscript prose, artifacts, interview answers, and AI output are not canon by placement or implication.

A new canonical assertion requires source evidence, review, an explicit author approval recorded in a decision, a canonical entry, and an index update. Record scope and exceptions so a book-specific truth is not silently made universal. See [the approval process](../docs/CANON_APPROVAL_PROCESS.md).

Maintain one authoritative entry per approved claim. Link to it from dependent story and manuscript records. A later correction needs a new decision, an impact review, and preserved history. Retired canon is marked Deprecated and linked to its successor, never silently deleted.

Approval metadata documents a human decision. A validator can check that fields exist, but cannot prove consent or determine fictional truth.
