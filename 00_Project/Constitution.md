# Constitution

## Purpose
Preserve a truthful development history and make controlled fiction writing possible across novels and series.

## Principles
1. The author is the final creative authority.
2. Preserve received originals unchanged; correct in supplementary records.
3. Source, inference, proposal, decision, canon and manuscript are different artifact classes.
4. Only `02_Canon/` contains approved fictional-world facts.
5. Uncertainty, contradictions, deliberate mysteries and rejected options remain visible.
6. Record a decision with provenance and consequences before canon promotion.
7. Each atomic approved fact has one authoritative home; other documents link to it.
8. Prevent cross-project or cross-series lore contamination.
9. Use stable IDs, indexes, checksums and reviewable commits.
10. Prose drafts and publication require separate author approval.

## Authority order
Explicit current author instruction → Constitution and policies → approved canon → approved architecture/decisions → active manuscript continuity → developmental notes → immutable evidence → labeled AI inference.

Changes to governance require an author-reviewed decision record. Nothing in this template constitutes story canon.
