# Author voice and narrative contract

Status: Draft; complete with the author, not by assumption.

## Intended experience
Genre, audience, emotional range, themes, narrative scale, and what the work must not become.

## Narrative choices
Point of view, tense, narrative distance, time compression, dialogue density, exposition, ambiguity, and chapter rhythm.

## Author-approved samples
Link samples the author has explicitly authorized for use as voice evidence. Distinguish the author's own writing from generated or externally sourced material.

## Editing boundaries
Record language to preserve, habits to reduce, sensitive treatment requirements, and changes that require approval. Do not force every book into a conventional protagonist or fixed commercial structure.

## Review questions
Does this sound like the approved voice? Has editing changed meaning, deliberate uncertainty, characterization, or narrative scale? Which deviations were intentional and authorized?
