# Project initialization interview

This is a starter question bank, not an obligatory questionnaire or story content. Adapt it to the author and avoid re-asking settled questions.

1. Is this an original book, rewrite, or series?
2. What is the central premise in your own words?
3. Why does the work matter to you?
4. Who is the intended reader?
5. What should the reader experience?
6. Which genres or forms describe the work?
7. What tone and voice must be preserved?
8. What narrative scale, viewpoint, and tense do you intend?
9. Which source files exist, and which named files may be inspected?
10. Which version is the author-confirmed comparison baseline?
11. What material must remain unchanged?
12. What is no longer working?
13. Which facts have explicit approval, and where is that evidence?
14. Which existing ideas are tentative or rejected?
15. Which mysteries should remain deliberately unanswered?
16. Which characters or relationships need clarification?
17. Which events or world rules have continuity problems?
18. What is the intended scope of this book versus later books?
19. What research, rights, or privacy constraints apply?
20. What bounded task should follow this interview?

Record numbered answers and statuses using the interview protocol. Do not invent answers or convert initialization into automatic prose generation.
